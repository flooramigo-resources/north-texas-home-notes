How to research Harris County expired or withdrawn listing leads
===============================================================

Before outreach about `Harris County expired or withdrawn listing leads <https://lienscraper.com/tx/harris-county/expired-listings/>`_, confirm the record and the property's current status. A listing that leaves the active feed is not automatically a sale.

Revisit the official record when the lead reaches your shortlist. Record status can change after publication; save the source and the date you checked it. Keep the source-backed fact separate from any estimate of seller motivation.

Prioritize properties where the recorded fact intersects your buying criteria, then document what remains unknown. Keep a separate note for observed facts and modeled economics.

For broader context, consult the `LienScraper property research guide <https://lienscraper.com/tx/harris-county/>`_.
