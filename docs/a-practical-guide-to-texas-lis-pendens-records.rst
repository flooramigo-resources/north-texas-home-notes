A practical guide to Texas lis pendens records
===============================================

Before outreach about Texas lis pendens records, confirm the record and the
property's current status. A lis pendens is notice of litigation that may
affect the property, not a judgment or a completed sale. Match the case number
and legal description, then check for a dismissal, release or later order
before interpreting its current effect.

Match the street address or legal description to the county appraisal account.
If the filing, owner and parcel do not agree, do not force a match. Keep the
source-backed fact separate from any estimate of seller motivation.

For a flip or wholesale prospect, evaluate condition, holding costs, title
questions and comparable sales before setting an offer. Neither an appraisal
value nor a former asking price is a confirmed sale price.
