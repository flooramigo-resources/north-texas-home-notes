A practical guide to Texas tax-delinquent properties
======================================================

Before outreach about `Texas tax-delinquent properties <https://lienscraper.com/guides/texas-tax-delinquent-properties/>`_, confirm the record and the property's current status. Separate delinquent taxes, a tax lien, a collection suit and a tax sale; they are different stages. Verify the parcel and tax years in the current county records, and request an updated payoff rather than relying on an old published balance.

Match the street address or legal description to the county appraisal account. If the filing, owner and parcel do not agree, do not force a match. Keep the source-backed fact separate from any estimate of seller motivation.

For a flip or wholesale prospect, evaluate condition, holding costs, title questions and comparable sales before setting an offer. Neither an appraisal value nor a former asking price is a confirmed sale price. See the `LienScraper property research guide <https://lienscraper.com/tx/>`_ for related research guidance.
