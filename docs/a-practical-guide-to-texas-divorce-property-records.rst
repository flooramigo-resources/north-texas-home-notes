A practical guide to Texas divorce property records
===================================================

Before outreach about Texas divorce property records, confirm the record and the property's current status. A divorce filing does not establish that the property will be sold. Check whether the real estate is identified in the relevant record, compare both parties with the current ownership record, and look for a later decree or deed.

Match the street address or legal description to the county appraisal account. If the filing, owner and parcel do not agree, do not force a match. Keep the source-backed fact separate from any estimate of seller motivation.

For a flip or wholesale prospect, evaluate condition, holding costs, title questions and comparable sales before setting an offer. Neither an appraisal value nor a former asking price is a confirmed sale price.
