A practical guide to Texas foreclosure property records
==========================================================

Before outreach about `Texas foreclosure property records <https://lienscraper.com/guides/texas-foreclosure-property-records/>`_, confirm the record and the property's current status. Distinguish a sale notice from a postponement, cancellation or recorded trustee deed. Confirm the current auction date and instructions with the official source, then check taxes, other liens and occupancy before estimating a bid.

Match the street address or legal description to the county appraisal account. If the filing, owner and parcel do not agree, do not force a match. Keep the source-backed fact separate from any estimate of seller motivation.

For a flip or wholesale prospect, evaluate condition, holding costs, title questions and comparable sales before setting an offer. Neither an appraisal value nor a former asking price is a confirmed sale price. For broader Texas property research, see the `LienScraper property research guide <https://lienscraper.com/tx/>`_.
