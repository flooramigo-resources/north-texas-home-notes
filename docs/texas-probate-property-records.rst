A practical guide to Texas probate property records
====================================================

Before outreach about `Texas probate property records <https://lienscraper.com/guides/texas-probate-property-records/>`_, confirm the record and the property's current status. Match the court filing and named parties to the property; an estate case alone does not prove the home is for sale. Check later case activity and who currently has authority to act before treating an heir or representative as a seller.

Match the street address or legal description to the county appraisal account. If the filing, owner and parcel do not agree, do not force a match. Keep the source-backed fact separate from any estimate of seller motivation.

For a flip or wholesale prospect, evaluate condition, holding costs, title questions and comparable sales before setting an offer. Neither an appraisal value nor a former asking price is a confirmed sale price. For broader context, see the `LienScraper property research guide <https://lienscraper.com/tx/>`_.
